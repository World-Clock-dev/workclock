Vacation Requests

Painters ask for days off from their Vacation page. Approve to record the hours as vacation taken, or deny with a note. They see the answer straight away.

Employee Vacations

From the week of Nov 30, 2026, vacation follows the performance guide: each week 0.77 × (paid hours ÷ 40, at most 1) × that week's performance score ÷ 100, at most 40 hours a year. It is provisional until you finalize the month in Performance. Vacation earned before that keeps the old rule (40 hours per 2,080 paid hours) and is never taken away.

EmployeeStart datePaid hours Earned Earned in totalManager adjustVacationBalance
Open shifts count once they are clocked out. Balance = earned in total (including hours earned before WorkClock and manager adjustments) − vacation taken. Use “Manager adjust” to fix a mistake: add or take away hours, with a reason. Paid vacation can be used after the first full year from the start date. Set start dates in Employees.

Settings

Clock-out radius and pay rules, data retention, and the manager password.

3 miles is the default clock-out radius. A normal shift reaching 7 hours 45 minutes is paid as a full 8 hours. Approved exception reasons can also be paid at 8 hours, subject to manager review.

WorkClock keeps a rolling window of time data. Shifts, rejected attempts, and audit records older than the window are deleted automatically each night. Employees, wages, titles, and settings are never deleted.
