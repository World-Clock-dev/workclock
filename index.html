Clock-out radius and pay rules, data retention, and the manager password.

3 miles is the default clock-out radius. A normal shift reaching 7 hours 45 minutes is paid as a full 8 hours. Approved exception reasons can also be paid at 8 hours, subject to manager review.

WorkClock keeps a rolling window of time data. Shifts, rejected attempts, and audit records older than the window are deleted automatically each night. Employees, wages, titles, and settings are never deleted.
